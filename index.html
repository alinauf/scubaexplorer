How to dive

You start at the edge of the reef. Swim out over the drop-off and you can keep going down through every ocean zone to the trench floor.

  • Mouse / ArrowsLook around. Click the water to lock the mouse (Esc releases it), drag to look, or use the arrow keys.
  • W A S DSwim forward, back and sideways in the direction you're looking
  • Space CRise and sink
  • ShiftDive fast (gets faster the deeper you are)
  • Click / ELearn about the animal in your crosshair
  • FRaise or lower your underwater camera. Click or press Enter to take a photo; scroll or +/− to zoom. The strobe fires automatically once it gets dim.
  • PPhoto log: every shot, graded ★–★★★. Subjects stay unidentified until you research them on land.
  • GFish guide: browse every species and summon any of them
  • VSwitch between first- and third-person view

Rush at shy fish and they scatter. Stay calm and curious animals like turtles, batfish and Napoleon wrasse come to you. Puffers inflate, garden eels hide in the sand, and sharks hunt smaller fish.
